Privacy Policy
What We Collect

During a monitored assessment, {{ platform_name }} records: your registered profile photo (captured once, before any assessment), periodic face-presence status (not raw video), browser interaction events (tab switches, focus changes, fullscreen exits, copy/paste, right-click), and your submitted answers.

What We Do NOT Store

We do not record or store continuous webcam video. Monitoring works by evaluating live frames in real time to detect status changes (face present / absent / multiple faces) — only that status, not the underlying image, is written to the monitoring log.

How Your Data Is Used

Monitoring events and your Integrity Score are used solely to assess the integrity of this specific attempt and to generate your result and any associated report. They are not used for any purpose outside the assessment and audit process.

Storage & Retention

Monitoring logs are stored in the platform's database, associated with your candidate account and this assessment attempt, for audit purposes.

Your Rights

You may request access to the monitoring events and integrity data associated with your account through your institution's designated contact.
