Identity Verification

You must complete identity verification before starting this assessment. Your registered profile photo will be used as your reference identity for the duration of the session.

Continuous Webcam Monitoring

Your webcam remains active for the full duration of the assessment. The system continuously checks for the presence of exactly one face. Absence of a face, or the presence of more than one face, is recorded as a monitoring event and may affect your Integrity Score.

Browser Monitoring

The assessment window monitors tab switches, loss of window focus, fullscreen exits, page refresh attempts, copy/paste actions, right-click usage, and — on a best-effort basis — developer tools usage. Each of these is logged as an event.

Fullscreen Requirement

The assessment is intended to be taken in fullscreen mode. Exiting fullscreen during the session is recorded as a violation.

Copy/Paste Restriction

Copy and paste actions within the assessment window are discouraged and logged as monitoring events.

Tab Switching Rules

Switching to another browser tab or application window is recorded. Repeated tab switching beyond the configured threshold contributes to a higher risk classification.

Violation Policy

Each violation type carries a point deduction from your Integrity Score, as described in the Integrity Policy step. Your final Integrity Score is stored alongside your result and may be reviewed.

Auto Submission Policy

Your assessment is automatically submitted when the timer reaches zero, regardless of how many questions have been answered. Unanswered questions are marked accordingly.

Privacy Notice

Monitoring data (webcam status transitions and browser events — not raw video) is stored securely and used only for integrity verification and reporting purposes. See the Privacy Policy step for detail.

Candidate Responsibilities

You are responsible for ensuring a quiet, well-lit, single-occupant environment, a stable internet connection, and a functioning camera before starting. Technical issues arising from your environment remain your responsibility.
